Apprenticeship

We can carry apprentices on public work.

Colorado public projects of $1 million or more must comply with the apprenticeship utilization requirements of C.R.S. §24-92-115, and the contractor has to submit apprenticeship certification for electrical work before the contract is executed. Only apprentices registered with a registered apprenticeship program count toward those requirements.

Colpitts Electric participates in registered apprenticeship program name sponsor / participant number, which means our apprentices attend related classroom instruction and accrue credited hours — and count toward apprenticeship utilization on qualifying public projects.

Verify before publishing: confirm the exact program name, that it is registered with the U.S. DOL Office of Apprenticeship or the Colorado State Apprenticeship Agency, and Colpitts’ standing as a participating employer. Do not claim apprenticeship utilization eligibility on an unregistered training arrangement.

The project

A large-scale institutional electrical buildout on a 104,207 sq ft campus serving a congregation of more than 2,000 — the kind of high-occupancy assembly space where electrical work has to satisfy life-safety, egress lighting, and code requirements that a standard tenant improvement never touches.

Accuracy note: the 104,207 sq ft figure describes the whole campus. Colpitts Electric holds the electrical permit for this work — state the permitted scope plainly in the panel above rather than letting the campus size imply it.

Our scope

What Colpitts actually performed — branch circuits, feeders, sanctuary lighting, lobby and coffee bar power, HVAC equipment connections, low-voltage pathways. Be specific and be accurate.

Working in an occupied assembly building

Phasing around weekend services, night or off-hours work, coordination with other trades, dust and access control in occupied areas.

Why large commercial buildouts are different

High-occupancy assembly space carries requirements most commercial tenant improvements don’t: emergency and egress lighting, life-safety circuits, dimming and control systems sized for a sanctuary, and dedicated power for AV, kitchen, and mechanical equipment.

Colpitts Electric is led by a Master Electrician with more than 20 years in the trade and five-plus years as a commercial foreman and superintendent — experience built on exactly this kind of coordinated, multi-trade commercial work.

The result

Outcome — completed on schedule, additional scope awarded, GC feedback. Required before this page publishes.
