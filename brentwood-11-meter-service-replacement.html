The site and scope

Replacing a multi-meter service takes more than new equipment.

Brentwood is a multi-tenant property in Westminster. Project correspondence identified failed bussing in the existing equipment and requirements for an eleven-position meter assembly.

01

Failed meter-stack bussing

Project correspondence identified failed bussing in an existing multi-meter assembly at the Brentwood property in Westminster, Colorado. The project called for a replacement approach suited to an eleven-meter service.

02

Metering and distribution

The replacement scope involved sourcing compatible multi-meter equipment and reviewing circuit-breaker options. Supplier lead times were part of the planning process.

03

Working with the utility

The project required utility documentation and planning for an electrical outage and reconnection. Coordinating those interfaces matters when replacing service equipment at a multi-tenant property.
