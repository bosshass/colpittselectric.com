About Colpitts Electric

A Woman-Owned Electrical Contractor Serving Denver Metro

Colpitts Electric is a woman-owned, SBA 8(a) certified, licensed and insured electrical contractor serving commercial builders, general contractors, property managers, business owners, home builders, and homeowners throughout the Denver Metro area.

The company was founded by Master Electrician Melissa Colpitts, who brings more than 20 years of hands-on electrical experience to every project.

Meet Master Electrician Melissa Colpitts

Melissa Colpitts

Owner / Master Electrician

Melissa began her electrical career immediately after high school as an apprentice. She worked her way through the trade, earned her Journeyman Electrician license, and continued advancing her education and field experience to become a Master Electrician.

Before founding Colpitts Electric, Melissa spent more than five years working as a commercial electrical foreman and superintendent. She was responsible for leading crews, coordinating with general contractors and other trades, maintaining project schedules, responding to changing jobsite conditions, and helping move complex commercial electrical projects from planning through completion.

That experience continues to shape how Colpitts Electric approaches every job today.
