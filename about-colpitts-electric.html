Melissa Colpitts

20+ years in the electrical industry.

Melissa Colpitts started in the electrical trade after high school as an apprentice. She earned her Journeyman license and went on to become a Master Electrician.

Before starting Colpitts Electric, Melissa spent five years as a Foreman and Superintendent running commercial jobs. That experience shapes how the company approaches scheduling, field coordination, safety and communication.
