Residential Electrical Troubleshooting

Flickering lights, frequently tripped breakers, dead outlets, inconsistent power, burning odors, buzzing devices, and warm switches can all indicate a problem that needs professional attention. We identify the source, explain what we find, and recommend the appropriate repair.

  • Breakers that repeatedly trip
  • Outlets or switches that no longer work
  • Flickering or dimming lights
  • Partial power loss
  • Damaged electrical devices
  • Overloaded circuits
  • Unusual electrical sounds or odors
  • Recurring electrical problems
  • General electrical safety concerns

Residential electrical FAQs

Common questions

Do you provide residential electrical service throughout Denver Metro?

Yes. Colpitts Electric serves homeowners, builders, remodelers, and property managers throughout the Denver Metro area. Contact us with the project address to confirm availability.

Can you troubleshoot an electrical problem if I do not know what is causing it?

Yes. Troubleshooting is one of our core residential electrical services. Tell us what you are seeing, hearing, or experiencing, and we will evaluate the system to identify the likely source.

Do you work with general contractors on residential remodels?

Yes. We can coordinate the electrical scope with general contractors, designers, homeowners, and other trades working on the remodel.

Can you replace or upgrade my electrical panel?

Yes. We provide electrical panel changes and upgrades based on the condition of the existing equipment and the electrical needs of the home.
