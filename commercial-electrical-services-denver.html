Why builders call us

Run by someone who ran the jobs.

Commercial electrical projects require more than technical skill. They require coordination, communication, scheduling, safety awareness, accurate work, and the ability to identify problems before they affect other trades or delay the project.

Melissa Colpitts spent more than five years as a commercial electrical foreman and superintendent before founding Colpitts Electric. She understands the expectations placed on an electrical subcontractor because she has worked both in the field and in commercial jobsite leadership.

Construction partners can expect clear communication, reliable scheduling, coordination with other trades, careful attention to plans and specifications, practical jobsite problem-solving, experienced electrical supervision, and workmanship built for long-term performance.

Our commercial services

Commercial Electrical Services

Nine core service areas covering the full life of a commercial electrical system.

Commercial New Construction

Complete electrical installation for new commercial buildings, including power distribution, lighting, controls, equipment connections, and coordination with project managers and other trades.

Tenant Improvements and Build-Outs

Electrical work for office, retail, industrial, and commercial tenant spaces — including new circuits, lighting modifications, equipment connections, panel work, and electrical system reconfiguration.

Commercial Remodels

Electrical upgrades and modifications for occupied and unoccupied commercial spaces, completed with careful planning to minimize disruptions to employees, customers, and daily operations.

Electrical Service Calls

Responsive electrical service for businesses experiencing outages, damaged components, equipment issues, lighting failures, or other electrical concerns.

Troubleshooting and Repairs

Detailed diagnosis of electrical problems, including recurring breaker trips, power loss, malfunctioning circuits, lighting problems, damaged devices, and electrical system performance issues.

Electrical Maintenance

Ongoing and scheduled electrical maintenance to help businesses reduce unexpected outages, identify potential hazards, and extend the life of their electrical systems.

Panels and Electrical Distribution

Panel replacements, panel upgrades, circuit additions, load evaluations, breaker replacements, and electrical distribution improvements for commercial properties.

Commercial Lighting and Controls

Installation, replacement, and modification of interior and exterior lighting systems, occupancy sensors, timers, dimmers, lighting controls, and energy-efficient LED upgrades.

Equipment and Dedicated Circuits

Electrical connections and dedicated circuits for commercial equipment, appliances, machinery, HVAC systems, and other specialized power requirements.

Commercial electrical FAQs

Common questions

Do you work as an electrical subcontractor for general contractors?

Yes. General contractors, commercial builders, and electrical contractors are a core part of our work. We carry the licensing and insurance your project requires and can provide certificates on request.

Can you perform electrical work in an occupied commercial building?

Yes. Commercial remodels and tenant improvements are planned around occupancy and coordinated with building management to minimize disruption to employees, customers, and daily operations.

Do you provide ongoing commercial electrical maintenance?

Yes. Scheduled and ongoing electrical maintenance helps businesses reduce unexpected outages, identify potential hazards, and extend the life of their electrical systems.

Can you install dedicated circuits for commercial equipment?

Yes. We provide electrical connections and dedicated circuits for commercial equipment, appliances, machinery, HVAC systems, and other specialized power requirements.

Do you provide additional electrical manpower on commercial projects?

Yes. Electrical manpower and subcontracting support is available for general contractors, electrical contractors, and commercial builders throughout the Denver Metro area.

What areas do you serve?

Colpitts Electric serves the Denver Metro area and surrounding Front Range. Contact us with the project address to confirm availability.
