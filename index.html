Why builders call us

Run by someone who ran the jobs.

Colpitts Electric was founded by Master Electrician Melissa Colpitts, who brings more than 20 years of hands-on electrical experience to every project — including more than five years as a commercial electrical foreman and superintendent.

She understands the expectations placed on an electrical subcontractor because she has worked both in the field and in commercial jobsite leadership: leading crews, coordinating with general contractors and other trades, holding schedules, and moving complex commercial projects from planning through completion.

That experience shapes how Colpitts Electric approaches every job today — commercial or residential.
